# Store listing: FORK BOMB

**Name:** FORK BOMB
**Category:** games  **Icon:** star  **Accent:** 9945FF
**Permissions:** led, lamps, http (host: forkbomb-scores.hypnogaba.workers.dev, the leaderboard)

## Short summary
Bomb-maze arcade on Solana: blast forks shaped like the Solana logo, dodge rugs, jeets and MEV bots,
12 power-ups, global leaderboard, daily !LAMPs.

## Description
Drop bombs, blow up forks and reach the Finality gate before your slot runs out.

- 8 stages across DEVNET, TESTNET, MAINNET and BREAKPOINT. Every stage is built around a picture
  made of breakable blocks: the Solana logo, DoubleZero's "00", GM, SOL, LFG, TPS and BP.
- Foes: RUG (slow), JEET (panics), SANDWICH (an MEV bot that charges you) and FUD (drifts
  through walls). Chain kills in one blast double the points.
- 12 power-ups: +1 BOMB, +1 RANGE, IBRL speed, SHIELD, KICK, REMOTE, FIREDANCER (flames pierce
  forks), MAX TPS, ZK PASS, AIRDROP (+1 life), +30 SLOTS, and OUTAGE (a curse: avoid it).
- Global leaderboard: your best score goes up after each game, with your X handle or as anon.
- Earn !LAMPs once a day: 1 for the first clear of each stage, 5 for finishing the epoch from
  1-1, 2 for a new best score (up to 15 a day, X linked).
- LEDs show your bomb fuse and lives. Clear 4-2 and a faster epoch begins.
- The title screen keeps a few secrets. gm.

Controls: D-pad move, SELECT bomb (hold it with REMOTE), CANCEL pause.

No wallet, no keys, no real money: only in-game !LAMPs.
